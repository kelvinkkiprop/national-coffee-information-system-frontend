Privacy Policy

Learn how NCIS collects, uses and protects your personal data.

  1. Purpose of The Policy

    This Privacy Policy explains how NCIS collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data in accordance with applicable laws.

  2. Whose Data May be Processed?

    NCIS may process data relating to coffee growers, farm representatives, cooperatives, millers, dealers, marketers, exporters, employees, public officers, verifiers, auditors and system users.

  3. Scope of The Policy

    This Policy applies to personal data processed through NCIS registration, farm geolocation, production records, licensing, traceability, verification, reporting, account management and technical support.

  4. Purposes of Processing

    Personal data may be processed to register and verify users, identify growers and farms, support traceability, manage accounts, validate records, facilitate regulatory oversight, generate authorized reports, investigate incidents and meet legal and audit obligations.

    Data will not be used for incompatible purposes without a lawful basis and any required notice or safeguards.

  5. Lawful Basis for Processing

    NCIS processes personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019, using an applicable lawful basis such as legal obligations, public functions, contractual necessity or consent where appropriate.

  6. Whether Providing Data Is Required

    Certain information may be mandatory for registration, verification or service delivery. Failure to provide required information may limit the services available.

  7. Sources of Data

    Personal data may be collected directly from users, authorized representatives, government agencies, official records, approved integrations and NCIS system logs.

  8. Sharing and Recipients of Personal Data

    Data may be shared lawfully with authorized government institutions, coffee-sector regulators, approved service providers, auditors and competent authorities where necessary. Data will not be sold or disclosed for unrelated commercial purposes without a lawful basis.

  9. International Transfers of Personal Data

    Transfers outside Kenya will be subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards, including documented purposes, recipients and lawful bases.

  10. Data Retention

    Personal data will be retained only as long as necessary for its intended purpose or as required by law, audit obligations and approved retention schedules. Unnecessary data will be securely deleted, anonymized or disposed of.

  11. Security Safeguards

    NCIS will apply appropriate security measures, including access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, audit logs, secure storage, backups, security monitoring and incident response. Users should protect their credentials and report suspected security incidents promptly.

  12. Data Subject Rights

    Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to, correction or deletion of their personal data, object to processing or exercise other applicable data protection rights by contacting the designated privacy contact.

  13. Automated Decisions and Profiling

    Where NCIS uses automated decision-making, profiling or risk scoring that significantly affects individuals, relevant information, potential consequences and applicable rights will be communicated as required by law.

  14. Personal Data Breaches

    Suspected data breaches must be reported promptly. The responsible institution will investigate, document and notify the relevant authority and affected individuals where legally required.

  15. Changes to This Notice

    This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in legislation, NCIS functionality or data processing practices.
