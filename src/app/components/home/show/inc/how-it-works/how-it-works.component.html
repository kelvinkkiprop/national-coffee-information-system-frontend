How It Works

Follow this step-by-step guide to understand how to register, submit information, verify records, track coffee and manage compliance through the National Coffee Information System (NCIS)

  1. Register an Account

    Click the Registration link, fill in the required user and company details, then submit the form. This will facilitate the online registration of stakeholder system users and organization details.

  2. Receive Login Credentials

    Upon successful registration, an email containing your login credentials will be sent to your registered email address to facilitate access to the portal.

  3. Sign In to the Portal

    Once you receive your credentials, log in to the National Coffee Information System (NCIS) portal using your registered email address and the credentials provided. This will enable you to access the system's available services.

  4. Access Services and Perform Tasks

    Once logged in to the portal, users can carry out the respective tasks and roles for the relevant directorates, depending on the functionality they intend to perform and the permissions assigned to their accounts.

  5. One-Time Registration

    Registration is a one-time process for users and companies. Once registered, users and organizations can continue accessing the portal and its available services without having to register again.
