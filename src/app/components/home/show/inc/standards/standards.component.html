Standards

The National Coffee Information System (NCIS) promotes compliance with established coffee quality, production, traceability, and market requirements. It provides a digital platform for accessing relevant standards and supporting transparency throughout Kenya's coffee value chain.

Our key standards:

1. Coffee Quality Standards

Promote consistent coffee quality through established grading, classification, processing, storage, and handling requirements.

2. Origin Verification and Traceability

Support verification of coffee origin and traceability across the value chain, from production and processing to marketing and export.

3. Production and Sustainability

Encourage responsible coffee farming practices, sustainable production, environmental stewardship, and adherence to applicable agricultural guidelines.

4. Processing and Post-Harvest Handling

Promote appropriate harvesting, drying, milling, storage, and transportation practices to preserve coffee quality and value.

5. Regulatory Compliance

Support compliance with applicable Kenyan laws, regulations, industry standards, and relevant international market requirements.

6. Data Accuracy and Information Security

Promote accurate, reliable, and secure digital records to strengthen accountability, reporting, and informed decision-making across the coffee sector.

Our Commitment

NCIS is committed to supporting a transparent, traceable, and quality-driven coffee industry by facilitating access to relevant standards and promoting compliance across the coffee value chain.

Note: Applicable standards and regulatory requirements should be verified against the latest publications issued by the relevant Kenyan authorities and recognized standards bodies.
